Featured 0-to-1 Product

GloveSense: AI Neuro-Rehabilitation Platform

Deployed Study

User Discovery & Problem

Standard clinical motor tests (BOT-2) only evaluate children before and after multi-week therapy, missing day-to-day motor learning. Children with ASD experience sensory overload, rapid fatigue, and motor carryover during repetitive clinic trials.

The Product Solution

An 8-channel inductive wearable glove paired with an interactive Dash/React serious-game dashboard that translates 14 hand gestures into gamified therapeutic exercises across four biomechanical difficulty tiers.

Core PM Decisions & Guardrails

  • Sensory-Safe Guardrails: Replaced stressful countdown timers and high-contrast visuals with mandatory neutral "C-Shape" rest phases and low-saturation background color washes to prevent sensory overstimulation and motor perseveration.
  • Edge ML Trade-off: Prioritized lightweight Random Forest classification on 8 energy channels over deep neural networks, maintaining sub-200ms local inference on low-power clinic laptops without dropped serial packets.
  • Validated Outcomes: 100% of cohort (14/14 participants) demonstrated improved Macro Accuracy (gains up to +0.63) and consistent drops in Intra-Class Distance, capturing subtle longitudinal progress missed by standardized exams.
