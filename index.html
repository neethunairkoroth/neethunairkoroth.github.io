GloveSense: AI Neuro-Rehabilitation Platform

Deployed Clinical Study

User Discovery & Problem

Standard clinical motor tests (BOT-2) only evaluate children before and after multi-week therapy, missing day-to-day motor learning. Children with ASD experience sensory overload, rapid fatigue, and motor carryover during repetitive clinic trials.

The Product Solution

Core PM Decisions & Guardrails

  • Edge ML Trade-off: Prioritized lightweight Random Forest classification on 8 energy channels over deep neural networks, maintaining sub-200ms local inference on low-power clinic laptops without dropped serial packets.
  • Validated Outcomes: 100% of cohort (14/14 participants) demonstrated improved Macro Accuracy (gains up to +0.63) and consistent drops in Intra-Class Distance, capturing subtle longitudinal progress missed by standardized exams.

BondAgent: Multi-Agent Fixed-Income Copilot & Yield Engine

AWS Bedrock & Serverless

User Friction & Market Problem

Institutional investors and fixed-income analysts face high cognitive load switching between disjointed market terminals to evaluate Treasury yield fluctuations, interest rate duration risk, and macroeconomic Fed policy shifts.

Multi-Agent Solution

Shipped an agentic conversational copilot on AWS Bedrock paired with an authenticated web interface, orchestrating specialized microservices for live bond pricing, duration/convexity modeling, and predictive yield scenarios.

System Architecture & Technical PM Decisions

  • Deterministic Tool Execution: Decoupled the Bedrock Agent into three dedicated Lambda action groups—Bond Data Generator (Alpha Vantage live ingestion), Technical Analyzer (calculating duration, convexity, and credit spreads), and Market Predictor (generating 30-day macro scenarios)—preventing LLM calculation errors.
  • Privacy-Preserving Authentication: Integrated Amazon Cognito with client-side SHA-256 session hashing, guaranteeing strict JWT verification and persistent DynamoDB conversation threads without storing raw user credentials in session state.
  • Resilient Ingestion & S3 Caching: Engineered a robust parameter extractor handling arbitrary Bedrock POST payload schemas, backed by S3 caching to eliminate third-party API rate limiting during high-volume queries.
